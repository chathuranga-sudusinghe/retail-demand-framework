## 3. Official website evidence

- **Author:** University of Greenwich
- **Title:** [New AI guidance to support learning and assessments in 2026/27](https://www.gre.ac.uk/articles/public-relations/new-ai-guidance-to-support-learning-and-assessments-in-202627)
- **Published:** 9 September 2026
- **Accessed:** 8 October 2026
- **Verification:** Public article inspected; this repository stores a source summary, not an archived copy of the webpage or complete policy.

The article describes three assessment tiers:

| Tier | Published meaning, summarised |
|---|---|
| 1 — Independent of AI | Restricted use to demonstrate the student's own knowledge, skills or judgement. |
| 2 — Supported by AI | Support for planning, clarification, feedback and refinement; the student retains responsibility. |
| 3 — Augmented with AI | Generative AI is intentionally incorporated into the assessment and critically evaluated. |

Staff determine the tier and assessment briefs specify permitted uses. The 2026/27 update calls for an AI Reflection (AIR), describing AI's contribution, the student's review/revisions and their own contribution, instead of the previously required declaration form. Students remain responsible for accuracy and originality; fabricated evidence is unacceptable. These statements are supported by the [published article](https://www.gre.ac.uk/articles/public-relations/new-ai-guidance-to-support-learning-and-assessments-in-202627), not by an independently inspected full policy.

The article links to [Responsible use of generative AI in teaching and learning](https://www.gre.ac.uk/docs/rep/information-and-library-services/responsible-use-of-generative-ai-in-teaching-and-learning). The full linked document could not be accessed during this review. Its detailed requirements are not verified here; obtain the applicable guidance through the university's channels before relying on details absent from the public article.

## 5. Recording actual AI assistance

For each member's relevant use, retain a concise factual account of the tool, task, affected work, output accepted or rejected, verification/revisions, and the member's own contribution. Link existing Issues, reviewed diffs, source checks and experiment records where useful. Record only use and review that actually happened; do not invent retrospective logs or claim generated text/code was entirely written by the student.

Use that account to prepare the acknowledgement required by the applicable assessment instructions. This note is not a completed declaration or AIR. The final reflective account and assessment permissions require human review; no lecturer or module-leader confirmation is asserted here.
