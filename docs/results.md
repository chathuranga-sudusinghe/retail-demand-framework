## 1. Evidence and scope

The project investigates how demand forecasting can be connected to inventory-risk information and transparent advisory records. The selected Kaggle dataset is simulated by its publisher, rather than observed from an operating retailer. Forecasting uses historical `Units_Sold` at `SKU_ID + Warehouse_ID + Date` grain. The [verified dataset profile](dataset.md#2-verified-dataset-profile) records 91,250 rows over 365 dates, covering 50 SKUs and five warehouses. The [analytical-unit decision](decisions/DR-002-forecasting-analytical-unit.md) preserves warehouse-specific demand and inventory context.

## 2. Research questions and findings

### 2.1 RQ1 — historical demand patterns

> What demand patterns can be identified from historical SKU-level sales data?

**Answer: descriptive coverage within the supplied dataset.** The retained [analytical-unit comparison](../reports/temporal-demand-profile.md#3-candidate-analytical-unit-comparison) shows complete daily histories, low median zero-demand frequency, variability and short-lag dependence at SKU-warehouse level. Aggregating across warehouses smooths demand but loses operational context: the [warehouse-alignment evidence](../reports/temporal-demand-profile.md#4-inventory-alignment-across-warehouses) shows that inventory level, reorder point and supplier lead time differ across warehouses within each SKU-day.

This supports retaining the warehouse dimension when connecting forecasts to inventory evidence. It does not establish that aggregation improves forecasting, that autocorrelation guarantees a model's suitability, or that the observed promotion differences are causal. The [dated-period synthesis](research-design.md#165-descriptive-comparison-of-dated-validation-periods--c1-follow-up) supplies descriptive high-demand, decline, low-demand and recovery contexts. These are not independently replicated demand regimes or evidence of a causal temporal-condition effect. Approximately one simulated year cannot establish robust long-cycle seasonality; see the [profiling limitations](../reports/temporal-demand-profile.md#9-limitations).

### 2.2 RQ2 — matched forecasting comparison

> Under the same forecasting inputs, temporal validation design, evaluation metrics, and matched hyperparameter settings, how do XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost compare in forecasting future retail demand?

**Answer: a completed descriptive comparison with horizon-specific ordering.** The [validation findings](forecasting-final-evaluation.md#3-validation-results) show broadly comparable primary-model performance. LightGBM has the lowest primary mean Weighted Absolute Percentage Error (WAPE) at the shorter horizons, and XGBoost at the longer horizons, but ordering changes across folds. The source's fold-level evidence therefore matters alongside mean errors; small aggregate differences do not establish significant or universal superiority. The [DR-013 interpretation policy](decisions/DR-013-matched-gradient-boosting-comparison.md#rq2-interpretation-and-claim-boundaries) preserves this descriptive reading.

In the same [validation findings](forecasting-final-evaluation.md#3-validation-results), supportive Random Forest has slightly lower mean WAPE than the primary leaders at 1 day, and contextual Seasonal Naive outperforms all learned models at 14/28 days; these observations retain their supportive/baseline roles and do not change the frozen producer decision.

The [final comparison](forecasting-final-evaluation.md#5-final-holdouttest-results) shows important counterevidence to a blanket preference for boosting: supportive Ridge has the lowest reported final WAPE at 1/7 days, and contextual Seasonal Naive at 14/28 days. They retain their supportive/baseline roles. All reported final Bias values are negative, meaning average underprediction under the recorded prediction-minus-actual convention. This does not itself explain individual inventory outcomes or establish business harm.

For every frozen producer, final WAPE exceeds its validation mean. The [validation/final comparison](forecasting-final-evaluation.md#6-validation-vs-final-holdout-comparison) compares different origins, training histories and target populations; it does not isolate a causal explanation for that difference. The [producer mapping](decisions/DR-014-forecasting-selection-and-final-refit.md#separate-downstream-forecasting-framework-producer-decision) was frozen before final evaluation and remains unchanged. A model's designation for serving is distinct from having the lowest final error. There is no approved cross-horizon overall ranking or final-result-driven reselection.

**Control of result-driven changes — confirmed by Chathuranga on 2 November 2026:** Final test and worked-scenario results were not used to change the frozen training plan, retune configurations, retrain models to improve those results or reselect designated producers. The approved final-refit procedure remains distinct from such result-driven changes. Underprediction and missed scenario crossings are retained and reported. This records a specific methodological control; it does not establish the absence of all research bias or remove the documented prior validation/EDA/outcome exposures. The worked example supplies case-level interpretation, not a new model-selection experiment or a diagnosis of the causes of forecasting error.

### 2.3 RQ3 — inventory-risk linkage

> How can forecasting outputs and inventory-state variables be combined to identify stockout, overstock, and replenishment risks?

**Answer: partial coverage through conditional reorder-threshold exposure.** The [approved DR-012 method](decisions/DR-012-inventory-risk-replenishment-methodology.md#group-approval-record) combines forecast demand with origin inventory and the reorder reference, holding that reference fixed and assuming no receipts or other adjustments. Source within-day inventory availability is unknown; the retained evaluation uses hypothetical constructed starting balances. It does not reconstruct actual future physical inventory.

The [retained report](../reports/inventory-risk/issue-164/evaluation-v1/report.md) compares forecast crossings with a retrospective proxy based on realised `Units_Sold`, keeping initially-at/below-threshold cases outside the paired comparison. The [horizon-specific metrics](../reports/inventory-risk/issue-164/evaluation-v1/report.md#horizon-specific-proxy-metrics) show why agreement alone is insufficient: h1 agreement is 0.9833 across 239 paired cases, while its missed-crossing rate is 0.4444 among nine proxy-crossing cases. High overall agreement can coexist with missed proxy events in an imbalanced population. The report retains each horizon's counts, rates and denominators separately.

The [margin interpretation](group-report.md#64-margins-unavailable-information-and-limitations) describes distance from the reorder reference under that scenario. A negative arithmetic margin is not verified physical negative stock. Retrospective targets, margins and metrics are evaluation evidence, not prospective inputs. The absence of a crossing does not establish overstock; a crossing does not establish a required order quantity or managerial-review trigger.

The current method approval covers h1/h7/h14; h28 inventory use remains gated. Numerical replenishment and overstock evaluation are disclosed limitations/deferred extensions under the [accepted scope](research-design.md#164-human-decisions-and-c1-closeout-record). `Stockout_Flag` is zero-variance and cannot validate actual stockouts. Consequently, this evidence answers the threshold-exposure part of RQ3, rather than validating stockout prediction, optimal purchasing or the full original inventory/replenishment ambition.

### 2.4 RQ4 — responsible use

> How can uncertainty, transparency, governance, and human oversight guide the responsible use of the resulting decision-support outputs?

**Answer: bounded record-level transparency evidence and documented governance, with interface acceptance separate.** The [retained criterion table](../reports/decision-support/issue-168-inventory-v1/summary.md) records forecast, assumption, limitation, human-review transparency and source-distinction checks as met in all 20 records. Risk visibility is met in 15 and not assessable in the five inventory-gated h28 records. Recommendation traceability and uncertainty visibility are not assessable in all 20. The source retains not-assessable cases in applicable denominators; those outcomes are not observed failures or evidence that the unavailable methods exist. No overall cross-criterion success percentage is inferred.

The practical contribution is to keep forecast provenance, scenario assumptions, limitations, missing-method reasons and advisory meaning traceable. The [presentation requirements](research/responsible-decision-support-evaluation.md#5-semanticpresentation-handoff-for-tinosh) preserve source-specific reasons and distinguish null/zero/false and unavailable information. Retrospective error is not a prospective uncertainty interval. `human_review_status=not_assessed` is not managerial clearance and is independent of completed transparency checks. The framework supplies information for human judgement without executing purchases or assigning an unapproved automatic trigger.

Chathuranga is the [recorded assessor and summary executor](research/responsible-decision-support-evaluation.md#32-human-run-summary-and-criterion-findings); this does not transfer Dewmi's component responsibility. Dewmi's [direct W1 confirmation on 1 November](https://github.com/chathuranga-sudusinghe/retail-demand-framework/issues/182#issuecomment-6555332904) accepts the interpretation/display baseline and treats automatic triggers only as a possible deferred extension. Her [final scoped W2 acceptance](https://github.com/chathuranga-sudusinghe/retail-demand-framework/issues/182#issuecomment-6704944020) supersedes the earlier pending update; her [#179 confirmation](https://github.com/chathuranga-sudusinghe/retail-demand-framework/issues/179#issuecomment-6724432473) records no remaining responsible-use blockers. This is not evidence of user trust, improved managerial decisions, regulatory compliance or real-retailer effectiveness.

## 5. Limitations and threats to validity

- **External validity:** publisher-simulated data and approximately one year of coverage limit transfer to real retailers and long-cycle seasonal inference. Source simulation assumptions are not independently validated; see [dataset limitations](dataset.md#11-dataset-limitation).
- **Temporal exposure and dependence:** the final interval had prior validation/EDA exposure, and the failed first final execution accessed outcomes before integrity completion. The corrected run retained frozen scope. Shared training histories, overlapping labels, selection-conditioned validation and one final origin limit generalisation; see [forecasting limitations](forecasting-final-evaluation.md#9-limitations).
- **Comparison scope:** matched external settings do not make different boosting algorithms internally identical. Supporting models/baselines retain their roles, and neither aggregate errors nor cross-horizon pooling supplies significance; see [DR-013](decisions/DR-013-matched-gradient-boosting-comparison.md#rq2-interpretation-and-claim-boundaries).
- **Inventory validity:** unknown snapshot timing, hypothetical balances, a fixed reorder reference and no receipts restrict interpretation. Actual stockout labels, optimal orders, service/cost improvement and operational inventory trajectories are not established; see the [inventory limitations](../reports/inventory-risk/issue-164/evaluation-v1/report.md#interpretation-and-limitations).
- **Assessment scope:** the purposive record roster is not population-representative or a controlled user study. Record transparency is separate from rendered usability, managerial review and decision quality; see [responsible-use reporting limits](research/responsible-decision-support-evaluation.md#4-current-findings-and-reporting-limits).
- **Deferred methods:** prospective uncertainty, numerical replenishment, overstock, automatic managerial-review rules and h28 inventory use remain disclosed limitations/deferred extensions under [C1's accepted scope](research-design.md#164-human-decisions-and-c1-closeout-record). Disclosure does not implement or validate those methods.

## 6. Overall conclusion and review boundary

The retained evidence supports a reproducible, horizon-specific forecasting comparison and a documented linkage from forecasts to conditional inventory exposure and transparent advisory records. It also exposes limits: simpler references can outperform the frozen producers, threshold-proxy agreement is not observed stockout accuracy, and transparency is not proven decision improvement. These qualifications are part of the result, rather than reasons to replace frozen methods or invent missing findings. The bounded conclusion follows the [accepted group synthesis](research-design.md#163-accepted-bounded-group-conclusion).
