# Chathuranga — COMP1885 Individual Project Overview

> **Boundary:** This is a planning overview only. The full COMP1885 implementation will not be developed inside the COMP1884 group repository.

## Proposed title

**Advanced Time-Series Demand Forecasting for Heterogeneous Retail Demand**

## Proposed research question

**How do temporal demand characteristics and demand regimes influence the performance and suitability of advanced statistical and machine-learning forecasting approaches in retail demand prediction?**

## Proposed hypothesis direction

### H0
Forecasting performance and relative model suitability do not significantly differ across defined retail demand regimes.

### H1
Forecasting performance and relative model suitability differ significantly across defined retail demand regimes.

## Research focus

The individual project extends the group forecasting component by moving from a general forecasting contribution to a deeper investigation of **forecastability and model suitability across heterogeneous demand behaviours**.

## Candidate demand regimes

Potential regimes include:

- stable;
- seasonal;
- volatile;
- intermittent;
- low-volume.

These must be defined empirically and/or through literature before testing.

## Possible advanced methodology

- deeper demand-regime characterisation;
- advanced lag/rolling/calendar features;
- statistical and machine-learning model comparison;
- rolling-origin/walk-forward validation;
- hyperparameter tuning without temporal leakage;
- error distribution analysis;
- forecast bias analysis;
- uncertainty estimation;
- explainability;
- statistical comparison across models/regimes.

## Expected product

An individual forecasting research prototype that:

1. characterises retail demand regimes;
2. trains/evaluates multiple forecasting approaches;
3. identifies where different methods succeed or fail;
4. communicates accuracy, bias, and uncertainty;
5. provides evidence-based guidance on model suitability.

## Relationship to COMP1884

COMP1884 establishes:

- the shared retail dataset;
- cleaning baseline;
- demand aggregation;
- a practical forecasting component;
- integration with inventory-risk analysis.

COMP1885 goes deeper into the forecasting research problem itself.

## New work beyond COMP1884

The individual project should add clearly identifiable depth, such as:

- formal demand-regime methodology;
- wider/advanced model comparison;
- more rigorous time-series validation;
- uncertainty quantification;
- statistical hypothesis testing;
- model explainability;
- deeper error analysis.

The final scope will be agreed with the supervisor.
