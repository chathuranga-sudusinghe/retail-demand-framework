# Dewmi — COMP1885 Individual Project Overview

> **Boundary:** This is a planning overview only. The full COMP1885 individual implementation will be developed separately.

## Proposed title

**Trustworthy and Explainable Retail Decision Support**

## Proposed research question

**How can explainability, uncertainty, robustness, and human oversight be integrated into a trustworthy retail forecasting and inventory-risk decision-support system?**

## Hypothesis status

A final H0/H1 is intentionally not stated yet.

Before a testable hypothesis is defined, the project must decide how to measure concepts such as:

- interpretability;
- robustness;
- transparency;
- user understanding;
- decision consistency;
- uncertainty communication.

A hypothesis should then be written around those measurable variables rather than around vague claims of "improvement".

## Research focus

This project would deepen the responsible-decision-support component into an independent investigation of trustworthy data-science practice in a retail forecasting context.

## Candidate methodology

- model/result explainability;
- uncertainty communication;
- robustness and sensitivity analysis;
- segment-level error/bias auditing;
- human-in-the-loop decision design;
- transparent decision rules;
- governance controls;
- scenario-based evaluation;
- potentially user/stakeholder evaluation if feasible and ethically approved.

## Expected product

A prototype responsible decision-support layer that combines forecast/risk outputs with evidence, uncertainty, limitations, explanation, and human review.

## Relationship to COMP1884

COMP1884 needs a practical responsible-use layer to complete the integrated group framework.

COMP1885 should go substantially deeper into how trustworthiness is operationalised and evaluated.

## New work beyond COMP1884

Potential deeper contributions include:

- formal transparency criteria;
- robustness testing;
- uncertainty-communication experiments;
- explanation comparison;
- bias/error auditing;
- human-review workflow;
- measurable evaluation of decision-support quality.

The final methodology must be agreed with the supervisor before implementation.
