# Didilani — COMP1885 Individual Project Overview

> **Boundary:** This is a planning overview only. The full COMP1885 implementation will be developed separately from the COMP1884 group repository.

## Proposed title

**Inventory Risk Modelling Using Demand Dynamics and Forecast Uncertainty**

## Proposed research question

**How can temporal demand behaviour, forecasting error, and forecast uncertainty be combined to model and evaluate inventory-risk proxies in retail operations?**

## Proposed hypothesis direction

### H0
Temporal demand dynamics, forecast error, and forecast uncertainty do not provide significant differentiation between defined inventory-risk proxy states.

### H1
Temporal demand dynamics, forecast error, and forecast uncertainty provide significant differentiation between defined inventory-risk proxy states.

This hypothesis is provisional until a valid target/risk definition and evaluation method are established.

## Research focus

The project deepens the COMP1884 inventory-risk component into an independent research investigation.

## Candidate methodology

- volatility measurement;
- intermittency analysis;
- demand-trend analysis;
- forecast-error and bias analysis;
- forecast-uncertainty features;
- segmentation / clustering where justified;
- risk scoring;
- classification only if a defensible target can be constructed;
- threshold and sensitivity analysis;
- business-impact interpretation.

## Expected product

An individual inventory-risk modelling prototype that transforms demand and forecasting evidence into transparent risk indicators, together with evaluation and sensitivity analysis.

## Relationship to COMP1884

COMP1884 creates an integrated risk component for the shared framework.

COMP1885 should investigate the risk-modelling problem in greater methodological depth and independently justify the variables, modelling strategy, thresholds, and evaluation.

## New work beyond COMP1884

Potential additions:

- formal risk-index methodology;
- uncertainty-aware risk scoring;
- multiple segmentation/modelling strategies;
- sensitivity/stability analysis;
- deeper evaluation;
- decision-cost or scenario analysis, if data and time allow.

The final scope will be agreed with the supervisor.
